Privacy policy

Notice the modal didn't pop? You arrived here without having consented yet, and showModal is on — but privacyHref resolves to this very page, so the auto-show is suppressed. A consent backdrop over the policy you came to read would be its own dark pattern. The explicit button below still opens the dialog on demand.

What this demo collects

Nothing. This is the example harness for @copperdesign/easy-cookie-consent. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party calls until you click an embed or accept in the consent dialog.

Third-party embeds

The demo embeds YouTube and Google Maps. Their iframes are never added to the page until you consent — so YouTube (Google LLC) and Google Maps (Google LLC) receive no request, and set no cookies, before that point.

Deferred loads

Google Fonts (Inter) is injected only after you grant global consent. Before then the page uses a local system fallback.

Your choices

Use the consent settings button above to review or change what you've allowed. Per-embed "remember" choices live in localStorage; a "Not now" dismissal is session-only.
