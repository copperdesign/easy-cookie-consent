easy-cookie-consent

A consent gate for third-party embeds. The YouTube iframe below is not in the DOM until you click. On first load, a modal asks whether to allow all embeds globally.

YouTube embed

Google Maps embed

Raw CMS embed (adopt)

A raw <iframe> as a CMS editor would paste it — no consent-embed class, no data-provider. consent.adopt() rewrites it into a gate, resolving the YouTube provider from the URL host. The host is never contacted until you click.

Deferred Google Fonts

Inter is configured via the googleFonts option below. Before consent, the line beneath renders in the page's Helvetica Neue fallback. Click "Allow all external content" in the modal — the stylesheet injects and the line repaints in Inter.

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.

The fonts also have a provider of their own. "Allow fonts only" calls consent.optIn('googlefonts') — the line above repaints in Inter while the embeds stay gated, and the onConsent callback below does not run.

Controls

"Reset consent" wipes localStorage for this origin and reloads the page — useful for testing the modal flow without opening a private window every time.

Privacy page (modal suppression)

privacyHref points to a real route (privacy.html), so the modal links there — and auto-suppresses once you're on it. Reset consent first, then open the policy: the dialog stays out of the way so you can actually read it. The privacy page still has a manual "consent settings" button, proving consent.show() works there.
